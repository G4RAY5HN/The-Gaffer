# The Gaffer — Master Drill Library

A big bank of solo/partner drills, organised by focus area, each tagged with the kit it needs.

**Assumed always available:** ⚽ ball · 🧱 wall
**Extras (tagged where needed):** 🚩 cones/markers · 🥅 goal · 🎯 rebounder · 👥 partner · 🪜 agility ladder

Format: **Drill name** — kit tags — short how-to.

---

## 🕹️ Touch & Control

- **Sole Rolls & the "V"** — Roll sole-to-sole side to side; then drag-back and push-out in a "V" with each foot. Metronome first, then speed up.
- **Toe Taps** — Light, quick taps on top of the ball, staying on your toes.
- **Foundations (inside-inside)** — Tap the ball between the insides of both feet, quick and controlled.
- **Bell Touches (front-back)** — Push the ball forward with the sole, pull it back — "ring the bell" rhythm.
- **Wall Passing (2-touch)** — 🧱 Control with one foot, pass with the other; control across your body into space.
- **One-Touch Wall** — 🧱 Return first time, alternating feet; start close, add pace, step back.
- **First-Touch-to-Turn** — 🧱 As the ball returns, take your first touch out and turn. Left = come infield onto the right foot; right = open down the line.
- **Half-Turn Receiving** — 🧱 Open your body as it arrives, back-foot touch, so you're already facing forward.
- **Juggling Ladder** — Thigh → foot → thigh, then feet only, then weak foot only. Daily PB.
- **Weak-Foot Juggling** — Juggle using only your left; rebuild balance and confidence.
- **Aerial Control (throw & kill)** — Throw the ball up (or off a high wall) and cushion it dead with foot/thigh/chest.
- **Chest-to-Foot Control** — Toss up, cushion on the chest, settle to feet.
- **Thigh Control Reps** — Pop it up, cushion on the thigh, reset.
- **Tight-Space Control (box)** — 🚩 Mark a small square; receive and keep every touch inside it.
- **Rebounder Aerial Control** — 🎯 Angled rebounder pops the ball up — control it out of the air, reset fast.
- **First-Time Return Rally** — 🎯 Fast one-touch rallies off the tight net; sharper than a wall.
- **Passing & Receiving (partner)** — 👥 Two-player passing; receive on the half-turn, vary distance, one and two touch.

## 🥅 Shooting & Finishing

- **Rebound Finishing** — 🧱 Take the wall rebound and strike; alternate placement and power, both feet.
- **Free Kicks — Driven** — Laces through the middle, firm plant foot, straight, no curl, top corner.
- **Free Kicks — Curled** — Across the ball with the instep, follow through across your body, over the wall into the corner.
- **Free Kicks — Disguised** — Alternate driven and curled with an identical run-up so a keeper can't set.
- **Volleys & Half-Volleys** — 🧱 Toss off the wall, strike on the drop; knee over the ball, keep it down.
- **Target Finishing** — 🥅 Finish into a real goal; aim corners, alternate near/far post.
- **Cutback Finishes** — 🥅🚩 Set a cone at the byline, "collect" and finish a cutback to the penalty spot.
- **1v1 vs Keeper (self-serve)** — 🥅 Push it, close the angle down yourself, side-foot or dink.
- **Long-Range Strikes** — 🥅 Strike from 20–25m; clean contact over power.
- **First-Time Finishing** — 🧱 Meet the rebound early before you'd set; get body over it.
- **Weak-Foot Finishing** — 🧱 Every finish with your left only.
- **Penalty Routine** — 🥅 Same run-up, pick your corner, repeat until automatic.
- **Crossing & Finishing (partner)** — 👥🥅 Partner serves crosses; attack near and far post.
- **Served Volleys (partner)** — 👥 Partner tosses, you strike on the drop, both feet.

## 🎨 Passing & Dribbling

- **Cone Slalom → Pass** — 🚩🧱 Dribble the slalom, release into the wall, change direction, repeat.
- **Stationary-Ball Weave** — 🚩 Tight dribbling through markers; small touches, both feet.
- **Dribble-to-Pass** — 🧱 Carry then release with accuracy; head up before the release.
- **Skill Moves** — Your cut-in chop, stepover-into-push, la croqueta; sell the fake with hips/shoulders.
- **Figure-8 Dribbling** — 🚩 Two cones, weave a figure-8 with close control, both feet.
- **Gate Passing** — 🚩🧱 Set two cones as a "gate"; pass through it off the wall for accuracy.
- **Long-Range Passing** — 🚩 Aim for a distant cone/target; driven and lofted.
- **Disguise Passing** — 🧱 Look one way, play the other off the wall.
- **1-2 Wall Combos** — 🧱 Play it in, move, receive the return, repeat — simulate give-and-go.
- **Speed Dribbling** — 🚩 Long touches at pace through wide cones; drive with the laces.
- **Passing & Receiving (partner)** — 👥 Vary distance and angle; one and two touch, both feet.
- **Give-and-Go (partner)** — 👥 Pass, sprint, receive the return; sharp one-two into space.
- **Third-Man Movement (partner)** — 👥🚩 Combine and move to a new cone; work the angles.

## 🛡️ Defending & Ball Recovery

- **Jockey Footwork** — Small steps, side-on; show the attacker wide / onto the weak foot, delay.
- **Close-Down Bursts** — 🚩 Sprint to press, break, jockey, repeat; recover goal-side each rep.
- **Shielding** — Body between ball and defender; roll away into space when pressure commits.
- **Recovery Runs** — 🚩 Sprint back goal-side from an advanced cone to a defensive one.
- **Defensive Slides** — 🚩 Shuffle laterally between cones staying low and balanced.
- **Interception Timing** — 🧱 Read the wall rebound and step in early to "intercept".
- **Block & Clear** — 🧱 Get your body/foot behind the rebound and clear it firmly.
- **1v1 Defending (partner)** — 👥 Live 1v1s; show them onto their weak foot, time the challenge.
- **Heading Duels (partner)** — 👥 Contested aerial reps; attack the ball, time the leap.
- **Press & Recover (partner)** — 👥🚩 Partner drives, you press then recover; game-realistic transitions.

## 🧤 Goalkeeping / Reactions

- **Wall Reaction Rebounds** — 🧱 Throw hard at the wall, set, then react to the rebound.
- **Rapid Reaction Rebounds** — 🎯 Fire into the rebounder, react to the unpredictable return.
- **Catch / Parry off Wall** — 🧱 Catch clean when you can, parry wide when you can't; body behind it.
- **Quick-Feet Reactions** — 🪜 Ladder patterns, then react to a rebound or call; front foot, fast turnover.
- **Low-Dive Handling** — 🧱 Soft throws low to each side; collapse-dive and hold.
- **High-Ball Claims** — Throw it up, attack the highest point, claim two-handed.
- **Distribution Reps** — 🚩 Roll/throw/kick to distant target cones for accuracy.
- **Shot-Stopping (partner)** — 👥 Partner shoots, you keep; set position then react, parry wide when needed.
- **Reflex Volley Saves (partner)** — 👥 Partner volleys from close range; pure reactions.

## 💪 Physicality / Conditioning

- **Shuttle Sprints** — 🚩 5–10–15m out and back; explode, decelerate, turn.
- **Sprint-then-Control** — 🧱 Sprint 10m, kill the wall pass, go again — match-fatigue realism.
- **Core Finisher** — Planks, side planks, glute bridges; form over reps.
- **Tempo Runs** — 🚩 Steady runs between wide cones to build engine.
- **Acceleration Ladders** — 🪜 Quick feet through the ladder into a 10m sprint.
- **Plyo Bounds** — Broad jumps / bounding for explosive power (land soft).
- **Agility T-Drill** — 🚩 Sprint, shuffle, backpedal around a T of cones.
- **Repeated Sprint Ability** — 🚩 6× 20m sprints on short rest; hold your times.
- **Ball-Carry Conditioning** — 🚩 Dribble hard between distant cones, both feet, on the clock.
- **Wrestle & Hold (partner)** — 👥 Shoulder-to-shoulder battles for strength and balance (controlled).

## Cool-Down Stretches (targeted by focus area)

The cool-down picks a **base set** plus **focus-specific stretches** for whatever you trained. Hold each 20-30s.

**Base (always):** calves - hamstrings - quads - ankle rolls & foot mobility (injured side extra)

- **Touch & control:** ankle & achilles - toe/plantar stretch
- **Shooting & finishing:** hip flexors - quads (striking leg) - groin
- **Passing & dribbling:** adductors/groin (side lunge) - hip openers (figure-4)
- **Defending & recovery:** groin (wide lunge) - hamstrings (single-leg reach) - hip rotators (figure-4)
- **Goalkeeping / reactions:** wrists & forearms - shoulders - lower back (twist)
- **Physicality:** hip flexors (couch stretch) - glutes (pigeon) - lower back & spine - full-body reach

## Notes for building sessions from this bank

- **Warm-up** (always first): jog + dynamic range (leg swings, lunges, ankle circles, calf raises) → quick feet & change of direction → light ball contact (sole rolls, toe taps). Wake the injured foot first.
- **Cool-down** (always last): light movement, then static stretch (hold 20–30s each), ankle/foot mobility, hydrate, log PBs.
- **Your-game flavour:** right foot sharp but left foot fed every block · left = cut in / right = stay wide on wall reps · head up more as the session goes on.
- **Post-injury:** warm the ankle every block, extra reps on the weaker side, respect soreness — stop a rep the moment your touch quality drops.
